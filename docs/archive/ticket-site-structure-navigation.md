# Site structure & navigation

## Question

What is Bahnhof's page structure? How is navigation organized?

- Known features: blog, song wall, navigation itself. Any other pages/areas?
- How does the "station" metaphor land on structure: signboard? timetable?
  waiting room? platform? (naming and structure inspiration)
- Navigation form: top bar? sidebar? station-map style entry?
- heart card wall's place in Bahnhof: Bahnhof is the navigation hub - do
  heart's cards (tayori etc.) migrate here?

## Background

- User: isui.ren/heart is frozen; navigation happens in Bahnhof from now on
- Bahnhof = station: blog, song wall, and records live here

## Resolution

Resolved via grilling with the user (2026-08-15):

- **Pages v1**: home + blog + song wall. Home is the station map.
- **Navigation**: station-map style - the home page lays out regions like
  platforms, each region is a page entered by clicking its platform.
- **Visual direction (major shift)**: NOT heart's white/grey aesthetic.
  Material You dynamic color derived from the user's browser/OS theme
  (Firefox/Chromium) plus iridescence/aurora accents. Realistic station
  vibe, not pure white & grey. A scrolling timetable element is welcome.
- **No tayori content**: the three balls stay out of Bahnhof - they belong
  to tayori's personal pages. Bahnhof is the owner's own station.
- **heart card wall**: untouched. heart is frozen as the tayori showcase
  plus the Bahnhof entry card. Bahnhof hosts its own content only.
